Refund Policy

All customers are eligible for a 30-day full refund at no extra cost. To request a refund, open the support page, enter the order number, and select "Request refund". Refunds are processed within five business days and appear on the original payment method.

If the item arrived damaged, attach the photo to the refund form and we skip the return-shipping step. Damaged-item refunds are usually processed within two business days.

Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for return shipping unless the item is damaged or incorrect. Use the prepaid label in your order confirmation email to avoid extra charges. Drop the package at any authorized carrier location and keep the receipt until the refund is confirmed.

Account Changes

To update the email on your account, sign in, go to "Account settings", then "Email". You will receive a confirmation link at the new address; the change takes effect after you click it. To change your password, use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page.

Order Tracking

Tracking links are sent by email when the order leaves the warehouse. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours after the label is created. If tracking has not updated after three business days, contact support with the order number.

Promotions

Seasonal promotions are announced on the home page and by email. Promotion codes are case-sensitive and cannot be combined with other offers unless explicitly stated. Stacking a non-stackable code will cause the order total to revert at checkout.

Loyalty Program

The loyalty program awards one point per dollar spent. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future orders at a rate of 100 points = $5. Points expire 12 months after they are earned if the account is inactive during that window.

Shipping Speed

Standard shipping takes 5-8 business days within the continental United States. Expedited shipping takes 2-3 business days. Overnight shipping is available for orders placed before 12:00 PM local time and is guaranteed by the next business day.

International Orders

International orders ship within 2 business days of payment confirmation. Customs duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the recipient. Refused international shipments are refunded minus the original shipping cost.
